Hibai Arbide Aza is a lawyer by training but works as a journalist. He has lived in Greece since 2014, where he has worked as a television correspondent and written for various international media outlets. He has been a member of Muzungu Producciones since 2015. For almost two decades, he has been involved in issues related to borders and freedom of movement: first as an activist, then as a lawyer, and now as a journalist, covering the so-called “refugee crisis” of recent years in Greece, the Balkans, the Middle East, and Germany. Other recurring subjects in his work include social issues, civil rights, and LGTBI+ matters. He hates writing his own bio.
