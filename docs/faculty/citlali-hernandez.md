Citlali Hernández is an industrial designer and digital artist with a Master’s in Digital Arts from Pompeu Fabra University. Her practice explores interaction, the moving body and identity through open technologies, digital fabrication and interactive systems. Her work has been presented internationally at events including ISEA, Ars Electronica Garden Barcelona, Loop Festival and the International Conference on Live Coding. She is part of the creative coding studio Axolot.cat, where she coordinates cultural projects at the intersection of electronic art and critical thinking, and is currently preparing a practice-based PhD.
