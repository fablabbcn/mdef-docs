Torres Bagó is formed by Sara Torres (Tarragona, 1994) and Èlia Bagó (Calella, 1994), and is based in Barcelona.

Their practice uses design to address what each context requires, whether real or fictional, particularly where industry or standard production processes fall short. Self-production and close attention to available modes of production play an important role in their approach, allowing them to maintain a material and ideological commitment to what they create. Their main areas of interest include the habitability of public and private spaces (Tangram, Eina Idea, Barcelona, 2021; SITIO, Barcelona, 2024) and support structures for contemporary art (La poesia acaba de començar, Fundació Joan Miró, Barcelona, 2025; Ple de forats, Centre d’Art Santa Mònica, Barcelona, 2023).

Torres Bagó also develops theoretical research exploring the relationships between power and the imaginaries of the home, the city and, more broadly, the built environment, through the material evidence embedded in architecture and design (La casa immaterial, 2024; Volum i escletxa, 2025).
