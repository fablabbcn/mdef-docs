Tim Cowlishaw is a British interaction designer, researcher and creative technologist based in Catalonia. His work combines critical, creative and technical approaches to explore digital technologies, their materiality and environmental impact. He works across research, design and development, with projects ranging from gallery installations and immersive experiences to websites, applications and physical prototypes. Previously part of the BBC’s Research & Development department in London, he has exhibited his work and led workshops and talks at Sonar+D, Eufònic, MediaLab Matadero and the Museum of the Moving Image in New York.
