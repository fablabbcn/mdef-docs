Director of Lichen Innovación Social and professor at the Autonomous University of Barcelona, he holds degrees in Business Management, Marketing and Branding. His work focuses on social innovation, citizen empowerment, open technologies, participation and experimentation. He has led and developed European and international projects at the intersection of technology, education, sustainability, mobility and digital inclusion, working with public, private, academic and social organisations across Europe and Latin America.
