Research consultant for several projects and institutions (Domestic Data streamers, FADFest, etc.) PhD in Sociology at the Autonomous University of Barcelona Bachelor’s degree in Philosophy at the University of Barcelona (BA Extraordinary Award). Bachelor’s degree in Economics at the Pompeu Fabra University. Toni Llàcer is a multidisciplinary researcher in Social Sciences with extensive academic and scientific experience. He has published several articles in impact journals such as Advanced in Complex Systems, Revue Française de Sociologie, or Revista Internacional de Sociología. He has participated in several international conferences at universities such as Columbia (USA) or Münster (Germany). He has been a member of the Analytical Sociology and Institutional Design Group (GSADI) and guest researcher at the Queensland University of Technology in Brisbane (Australia).
