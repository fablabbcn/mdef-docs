Hybrid Ecologies of the Llobregat Delta is a transdisciplinary research project that promotes initiatives in artistic creation, mediation, and experimentation. The group situates its projects in the Llobregat Delta, a rich and fragile naturcultural biotope located just 15 km from the centre of Barcelona, precisely where the city’s airport is situated. This wetland and marsh ecosystem provides habitat for various threatened species of birds, mammals, reptiles, amphibians, and fish, protected under Catalonia’s Plan for Areas of Natural Interest (PEIN) and the European Natura 2000 network. It is also a strategic site along the migratory routes of birds travelling between Africa and Europe. At the same time, the Delta is the most important peri-urban agricultural area in the Barcelona Metropolitan Area, a role it has maintained since the discovery, at the end of the 19th century, of a freshwater aquifer beneath the Delta. Despite its ecological value, the biodiversity of the Llobregat Delta is severely degraded by the expansion of numerous infrastructures, pollution, salinisation, mass tourism, real-estate speculation, and gentrification.
