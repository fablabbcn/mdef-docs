Santiago Fuentemilla is an architect and researcher with a Master’s degree in Architecture from La Salle – Universitat Ramon Llull. He graduated from the Fab Academy at Fab Lab Barcelona in 2012 and is currently pursuing a PhD in digital manufacturing at UPC. Since 2013, he has been part of the Fab Lab Barcelona core team, where he coordinates the Future Learning research area and directs the global Fab Academy and Fabricademy programs in Barcelona. He has been a professor in the Master in Design for Emergent Futures (MDEF) since 2017 and has participated in numerous EU-funded research projects exploring digital fabrication, education, STEAM learning, entrepreneurship, sustainable fashion and the circular economy.
