Andrea Santi is an Italian designer and maker based in Barcelona. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Product Design from the University of Bologna (Alma Mater Studiorum), where he soon realised that his way of designing was rooted in practice, hands-on experimentation and learning by doing. Alongside his studies, he was part of Blocco Comune, a maker space run by young designers inside DumBO (Distretto Urbano Multifunzionale di Bologna). That experience shaped his idea of the lab as an open, shared and collective place. Feeling distant from the more traditional Italian academic approach to design, he moved to Barcelona to join the Master in Design for Emergent Futures (MDEF) at IAAC and Elisava. There he developed a critical design methodology grounded in making, where fabrication is not only a way to produce objects but also a way to think, question and test ideas. Today he works as a maker and digital fabrication expert, moving between 3D printing, CNC milling, laser cutting, plastic recycling with Precious Plastic, carpentry and some welding. He combines these technical skills with critical thinking, and brings a strong ability to generate team energy and turn collective experimentation into tangible outcomes. His projects and experiments can be found at desainz.net.
