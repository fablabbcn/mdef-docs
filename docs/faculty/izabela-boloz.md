Designer, curator, educator. Director and owner of Studio Boloz - a design studio producing a wide variety of public space projects. Main focus: placemaking, multifunctional meeting spaces, urban installations, play installations, play sculptures, public art projects.

Curator of interdisciplinary exhibitions. Lecturer at the Technical University Eindhoven in The Netherlands and School of Form in Warsaw, Poland.

Background in social science (University of Wroclaw, Poland; University of Zurich, Switzerland) and design (Kingston University London, UK; Design Academy Eindhoven, NL).
