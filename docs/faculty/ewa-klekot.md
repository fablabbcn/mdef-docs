Ewa Klekot is an art anthropologist and translator, with a background in archaeology and ethnography and a Ph.D. in art studies. Her work explores interdisciplinary connections between the humanities, social sciences, design, and artistic practice. She led the research project "People from the Porcelain Factory" (0264/NPRH4/H2b/83/2016) and, together with ceramic artist Olga Milczyńska, co-created the exhibition "Robić/Rzeczy" at Łódź Design Festival 2019. Her current research focuses on the anthropology of making, including skills, embodied knowledge, materials, and processes as well as the relationship between craft traditions and intangible heritage.
