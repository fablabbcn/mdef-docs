Christian Alonso is a researcher, curator, and educator specialising in art and ecology. He is a Lecturer in Contemporary Art History at the University of Lleida (professor lector / assistant professor) and director of the artistic research project Hybrid Ecologies of the Llobregat Delta. Previously, he was director of Centre d’Art La Panera, associate professor at the University of Barcelona, adjunct professor at the ESDI School of Design–Ramon Llull University, collaborating professor at the Open University of Catalonia (UOC), coordinator of the curatorial studies programme On Mediation, and project coordinator for the research group Art, Globalisation, Interculturality at the University of Barcelona. He holds a degree in Art History and a Master’s degree in Advanced Studies in Art History from the University of Barcelona. He has received grants from the Ministry of Economy and Competitiveness of the Government of Spain and from the Office for Support of Cultural Initiatives (OSIC) of the Department of Culture of the Government of Catalonia. He has been awarded curatorial commissions through open calls by Centre d’Art La Capella, Centre d’Art Maristany, Can Felipa Arts Visuals, and Sala d’Art Jove. He has extensive experience in the research, teaching, management, coordination, production, and communication of curatorial, mediation, and artistic production projects. He is a member of the Catalan Association of Art Critics (ACCA) and Càlam. Art, Education, Sustainability.
