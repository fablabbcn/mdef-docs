 Damian Arezzo is a maker and entrepreneur based in Barcelona. He develops Woody.bio, a material made from sawdust waste and starch, without petrochemicals or toxic substances.
He leads the BioLab at Fab Lab Barcelona / IAAC, where he combines digital fabrication, tinkering and creative thinking to address real-world challenges.
His work is guided by a triple-impact approach, exploring how materials and systems can generate economic, social and environmental value simultaneously.
