## Schedule

=== "23/11"

    **Concept and Physical Prototyping**
    
    Definition of the wearable concept and development of its bodily input. Students construct and calibrate DIY sensors and explore physical integration through textiles, straps, elastic systems, flexible structures and digitally fabricated components. (*TBC Santi / Citlali / Fab Team*)

=== "24/11"

    **Programming and Data Connectivity**
    
    Programming microcontrollers (FabXiao / Barduino), reading and calibrating sensor data, and establishing relationships between inputs and outputs. Devices are connected to a shared digital environment through wireless communication, enabling individual data to contribute to visual, sonic or collective responses. (*TBC Dani / Hannah / Fab Team*)

=== "25/11"

    **Integration and Activation**
    
    Final integration of sensor, electronics, power, connectivity and wearable structure. Prototypes are tested on bodies and in relation to one another through performative situations, collective rituals and experimental protocols. (*Santi / Fab Team*)
