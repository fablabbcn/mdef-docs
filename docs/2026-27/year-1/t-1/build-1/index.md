## Schedule

=== "26/10"

    **From Experiment to Structure:** Development of material experiments using textile remnants and technical fabrics, including ripstop or equivalent lightweight material, into real-scale propositions. Definition of geometry, structural logic, pattern, joints and fabrication strategy. (*Santiago Fuentemilla / Fab Team, IAAC*)

=== "27/10"

    **Full-scale Fabrication:** Development of the prototype through pattern-making, full-scale video projection, cutting, sewing, reinforcements and assembly. Particular attention is given to the relationship between material properties, construction details and structural performance. (*Santiago Fuentemilla / Fab Team, IAAC*)

=== "28/10"
    
    **In-house Assembly and Testing:** Integration and testing of tensioning, inflation or wrapping systems. Full-scale testing with bodies, loads and spatial conditions, followed by iterative adjustments in preparation for deployment on site. (*Santiago Fuentemilla / Fab Team, IAAC*)
