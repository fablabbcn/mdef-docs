## Schedule

=== "19/10"

    Èlia and Sara / Torres Bagó

    **Site, Techniques and Design Development:**
    
    The first day will introduce the participants to the design studio’s practice, with a particular focus on the construction of textile     canopies and lightweight spatial structures. Selected examples from previous projects, such as Rizomes 2026 and H2O 2026, may be presented to illustrate relevant techniques, materials and construction principles.
The main objective of the day is to establish the basis for the intervention that will be developed and built during the workshop: a large textile canopy or shelter combined with a system that allows people to sit, lie down and comfortably occupy the installation. The proposal will be developed specifically in relation to the outdoor space at Cal Negre, taking into account the existing conditions of the site: trees, soil, porch.
The group of 30 participants will divide into smaller working groups and begin developing the proposal collectively. The design process will address questions such as whether the canopy should be conceived as a single continuous structure or as a series of connected elements, how many parts it should include, where the areas for sitting or lying down should be located, what form they should take, and how these elements should connect to the canopy.
Participants will work considering how the proposal can be realistically positioned, tensioned and constructed on site. The first day should also include initial full-scale tests.
    

=== "20/10"

    Èlia and Sara / Torres Bagó

    **Construction, Testing and Material Development:** 
    
    The second day will focus on translating the proposal into a full-scale construction. The group will begin with a defined plan of action. This stage will involve resolving the technical aspects of the proposal, including fabric tension, the geometry produced once the textile is stretched, air circulation, structural behaviour, and the connection of the installation to trees and other possible anchoring points within the site. A significant part of the morning will involve cutting, sewing, joining and tensioning textile elements, as well as testing components at full scale.
The objective of the second day is to understand how the initial proposal changes when confronted with material, structural and construction requirements, and to develop the installation through an iterative process of making and testing.

=== "21/10"

    **Adjustment, Completion and Collective Review:** 
    
    The third day will be dedicated to completing the installation and responding to the issues identified during the previous day’s     construction.Depending on the progress achieved on Day 2, the morning may focus on refining details, improving connections, adjusting tensions, modifying the seating or lying areas, and resolving smaller spatial or technical issues. If more substantial problems have emerged during construction, the day will instead be used to reconsider, remake or adapt the necessary parts of the structure.
The aim is to complete the installation by the end of the morning, leaving time for a collective discussion and review of the process, the decisions made throughout the three days, and the relationship between design, construction and site.
     
     Èlia and Sara / Torres Bagó

## Evaluation Criteria

**1. Critical and situated technological literacy**

Decoding the foundations of contemporary technology, including systems thinking, infrastructures and architectures, political economy, IP models and planned obsolescence, to develop a critical position that informs design practice.

**2. Exploration and reframing of emergent and alternative technologies**

Engagement with emergent technologies from research and industry alongside alternative ones, including deprecated, marginal, vernacular and analogue technologies, considering their narratives, applications and implementation, and recombining them to open new design possibilities.

**3. Collaborative Practice**

The contract documents clearly how knowledge, references, tools or skills were sourced, shared or developed with others, demonstrating an ability to learn from existing practices and contribute findings back to the group.
