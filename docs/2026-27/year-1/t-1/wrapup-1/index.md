Each trimester concludes with a dedicated **Wrap Up session** focused on individual reflection, feedback and integration.

Rather than a conventional final review, the session is structured around 1-to-1 conversations between each student and one of the tutors responsible for the different modules. These conversations provide a space to review the student's work, progress and experience throughout the trimester, identify challenges and opportunities, and reflect on the direction of their practice.

Particular attention is given to the development of each student's hybrid profile as a designer: how the different topics, methodologies, tools and techniques explored throughout the trimester can come together and contribute to a distinctive practice. Students are encouraged to connect what they have learned across modules, identify relationships between different areas of knowledge, and consider how these learnings can be translated into their evolving portfolio and future projects.

The Wrap Up therefore closes each trimester while also opening the path towards the next one, helping students understand where they are in their development and how to build on the experience ahead.
