**Kickoff 1 is the first week of the MDEF journey**: a time to land in the programme, get to know its environment, and connect with the people, spaces and practices that shape the Master.

During the first days, students are introduced to the physical and human ecosystem of IAAC–Fab Lab Barcelona and ELISAVA. They discover the spaces and facilities, meet the programme directors, coordinators and faculty connected to each module, and get familiar with the different communities that make up the MDEF environment.

The week also introduces the structure of the Master, its methodologies and ways of working, together with a detailed overview of the first trimester and its agenda. It is also an opportunity for the new cohort to get to know each other and connect with MDEF students from the second year, beginning to build the peer network that will accompany them throughout the programme.

The second part of the Kickoff focuses on digital fabrication. Through an intensive introduction to the fundamental tools, machines and processes of the Fab Lab, students develop the basic skills and confidence needed to start using the laboratories autonomously and to integrate fabrication into their design practice from the beginning of the Master.
