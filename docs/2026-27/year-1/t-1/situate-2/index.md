    **Exercise for 6/11:**

    **Identify 5 stereotypes:** who is expected to behave in a certain way? How are stereotypes produced and perpetuated? Who embodies them? What makes you think so? List clothing, behaviour, dancing skills, body odor, dilated pupils or any other elements you find relevant.

    **Identify 5 body-technology interactions:** sound systems, light, phones, screens, algorithms, infrastructures, systems, toilets, smoke, ventilation, etc. How tech infrastructures choreograph bodies and behaviours?

    **Identify 5 forms of pleasure:** sensory, bodily, chemical, social, performative, sonic, sexual, visual, etc. Who has access to pleasure? What protocols enable pleasure?

    **Produce 5 bizarre moments:** Propose and activate disruptions, individual or collective malfunctions, unexpected behaviours, body to body and body to tech interactions as a research method into the dynamics and ecosystems of the night. Document them through pictures, short videos, voice recordings, etc.
